All filters here narrow the collection by rarity dimension. Numbers next to each pill = count of uPEGs in that bucket.

  • ID — jump directly to a specific uPEG by number.
  • Market — listing status. Listed = anywhere; OTC = p2peg only; OpenSea = OpenSea only.
  • Notable — Full set = all 7 traits filled in; Naked = none of the 7 optional traits set.
  • Trait tier — how rare the trait combination is. 1 = most common, 4 = rarest (often just a handful).
  • Color tier — how dominant the top color is. 1 = balanced palette, 4 = one color dominates.
  • Distinct colors — how many different hex colors the uPEG uses (2–7).
